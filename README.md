# NetWorth SG 📊
Welcome to #ThatFinancialGal web-app personal networth tracker :)

Singapore-focused personal net worth tracker.
Track CPF, SRS, investments, property and retirement in one place.

## Features
- CPF breakdown (OA / SA / MA / RA)
- Multi-currency with FX support
- Retirement calculator with CPF Life
- Monthly trend charts
- 100% private — data stays on your device

## How to use
1. Open the link in Safari on your iPhone
2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen
3. Tap + Month to add your first entry

## Data & Privacy
All data is saved locally in your browser.
Export a JSON backup monthly from Settings.
